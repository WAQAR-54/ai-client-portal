Manager Guide

AI Client Portal — for Managers

Everything a User can do, plus your own dashboard for watching your team's usage and controlling which models they can reach.

Overview

As a Manager, you get everything a regular User has — chat, Compare mode, your own usage tracking — plus a dashboard scoped to your own team, where you can see how your team is actually using the app and adjust their model access.

What you can't do: suspend an account, change someone's role, or move a person to a different plan — those stay with your Admin. Everything you do here is either additive (granting access) or restrictive (narrowing it), never account-changing.

My Team

My Team

Team dashboard

Your team roster with each member's token usage, cost, and an engagement score, plus team-wide totals for all time and the current month.

Model access, two ways

You have two separate, independent levers over which models your team can actually use:

Team-wide restriction — turn off a model for the whole team. This can only make your team's access narrower than what their Plan already allows, never wider.

Per-person grants — for individual members, hand out access to models from a pool your Admin/SuperAdmin has separately marked "manager-assignable." This is independent of the team's Plan, and a personal grant wins over the team-wide restriction for that one person.

Remove a team member

Removes someone from your team's roster only. Their login, plan, permission history, and past conversations are completely untouched — they simply stop being counted as part of your team.

Chat

The main screen for actually talking to a model — identical to what a regular User sees.

Starting and managing conversations

New conversation, pin, search, delete

The sidebar lists every past conversation, grouped by date, with the most recent at top. Pinned conversations get their own section above the rest so they never scroll out of reach.

How: click New conversation to start fresh. Hover any conversation for the pin and delete icons. The search box above the list filters by title as you type.

Choosing a model

Model picker & Smart Routing

By default the app picks a model automatically for every message, based on rules your admin set up. You can override this and pick a specific model instead.

How: open the model dropdown at the top of the chat, or the quick-pick chips above the message box.

Compare mode (Arena)

Send one prompt to two different models at once and see both replies side by side.

How: the Chat/Compare switch above the message list, pick two models, and send.

Working with content

File attachments, export, and templates

Attach files (PDF, Word/Excel, text, CSV, Markdown, JSON, images) via the paperclip icon. Export a conversation as PDF, Markdown, or plain text from its export menu. Insert a saved prompt with / as the first character in the message box, or save your own from the Templates panel.

Edit, regenerate & feedback

Edit an earlier message to re-send and regenerate everything after it. Regenerate replaces one reply with a fresh attempt. Rate any reply with thumbs up/down, visible to your Admin.

Keeping track of usage

Usage widget & upgrade requests

The Usage pill at the top of chat shows your own plan usage. Request upgrade files a request straight to your admin.

Code Playground & Domain Generator

Two focused, single-purpose tools, separate from chat — reached by a direct link, not the main sidebar, and switched on or off per role by your admin.

Code Playground

Pick a model, describe what the code should do, and run it in a simulated sandbox. Counts against a daily quota (20 by default).

Domain Generator

Describe a business idea, optionally filter by TLD, and get AI-suggested domain names checked for real availability. Same daily-quota pattern.

Security & login

Password reset

Request a reset from the login screen, follow the emailed link to set a new one.

MFA (email one-time code) — optional for you

A 6-digit code emailed at login, valid for 10 minutes. Entirely optional for Managers.

How: Settings → Security.

Account lockout

5 wrong password attempts locks your account for 20 minutes. One successful login resets this.

Notifications
