Admin Guide

AI Client Portal — for Admins

Running your department: users, teams, spending limits, and the reporting you need — plus everyday chat access for yourself.

Overview

As an Admin, you manage everything about your own department — who's in it, what they're allowed to do, how much they can spend, and what actually happened. You can also do everything a Manager and User can (chat, and if you set up a team, manage it too).

Where your power stops: your view and control are scoped to your own department. You cannot see or touch another department's users, budget, or settings — and you can only promote/demote someone between User ↔ Manager, never grant Admin or SuperAdmin. Connecting AI providers, defining plans, and org-wide settings are SuperAdmin-only.

People & access

Everything scoped to your own department.

Admin → Users

Users

The roster for your department. Search and filter by role, active/suspended status, or plan. Each row shows their current plan, an engagement score, how many personal overrides they have, and recent request volume.

How: add a new account, edit role/email/team, reset a password directly (no email round-trip needed), suspend or reactivate an account, and change one person's plan — or select several rows and change plan in bulk. Clearing a user's personal overrides resets them back to whatever their Plan and department rules would otherwise give them.
Admin → Teams

Teams

Create or delete teams within your department — a Manager needs a team to exist before they have anything to manage. Shows each team's member count and manager.

Plans & money

What you can adjust for people in your department — you can't create new plans (that's SuperAdmin), but you can fine-tune limits and handle upgrade requests.

Admin → Limits

Limits

Per-user or per-department overrides layered on top of whatever the Plan already sets — daily/monthly token caps, session message limits, a monthly spend cap, and file-upload size/type rules.

Admin → Upgrade Requests

Upgrade Requests

The queue of "please upgrade my plan" requests your team files from the chat usage widget. Approve one to jump straight to that person's row on Users and change their plan, or dismiss it.

Monitoring & logs

Everything you need to answer "what happened, and how much did it cost" for your department.

Admin → Usage & Cost

Usage & Cost

The spend dashboard: a per-user table of requests, tokens, and estimated cost (filterable by user, model, date range), a weekly cost trend, and spend by provider. Exportable as CSV or Excel.

Admin → Audit Logs

Audit Logs

A searchable trail of every admin action taken in your department — who did it, what it was, what changed from and to, and when. The first place to check when something changed and nobody's sure who did it.

Admin → Feedback

Feedback

Every thumbs up/down and comment your team has left on AI replies, filterable by rating, model, or user.

Chat

You use the same chat screen as everyone else in your department.

Model picker, Compare mode, attachments, export, templates

Pick a model manually or let Smart Routing choose one automatically. Compare mode sends one prompt to two models side by side. Attach files via the paperclip icon, export a conversation as PDF/Markdown/text, and use / to insert a saved prompt template.

Usage widget

The Usage pill at the top of chat shows your own plan usage — as an Admin you're also on a plan like everyone else.

Code Playground & Domain Generator

Two focused, single-purpose tools, on by default for Admin/SuperAdmin. Reached by a direct link — not the main sidebar.

Code Playground

Pick a model, describe what the code should do, and run it in a simulated sandbox.

Domain Generator

Describe a business idea and get AI-suggested domain names checked for real availability.

Security & login

Password reset

Request a reset from the login screen, or reset a team member's password directly from Users — no email needed either way.

MFA (email one-time code) — mandatory when your SuperAdmin turns it on

A 6-digit code emailed at login, valid for 10 minutes, with a resend option and a 5-attempt limit before login starts over.

Important: unlike a User or Manager, you cannot opt out of MFA whenever your SuperAdmin has switched "MFA required for Admins" on (Feature Visibility). If it's off, MFA is simply unavailable to you either way — it's an org-wide switch, not a personal one, for your role.

Account lockout

5 wrong password attempts locks the account for 20 minutes. One successful login resets this.
