What the AI Service Is

{{ site_branding.site_name }} is a software platform we operate. It gives you a single workspace in which you can chat with AI models, compare their answers, work on documents and code, organise conversations into projects and manage usage and billing for your organisation. We provide our own software platform and AI-powered functionality through integrations with third-party AI service providers. The AI models themselves are built and run by those providers; the platform around them — accounts, access control, plans, limits, conversations, projects, exports, notifications, invoicing and administration — is ours.

Users access supported AI models through our platform. We do not sell, transfer, sublicense, or provide users with third-party provider API keys or provider accounts, and users do not receive credentials to any provider. What you get from us is access to features of our platform, according to your plan.

How AI Requests Work

When you send a message, the platform first checks that you are signed in, that your plan and role allow the feature, that you are within your usage limits and that the model you selected (or the model our routing chooses for you) is enabled for you. It then assembles the request — your prompt, the earlier messages of the conversation that give context, any file content you attached, and any instructions configured by your organisation — and sends it over the provider’s API to the third-party AI provider that runs the model. The provider returns a response, which the platform streams back to you and stores in your conversation together with the model used, token counts and an estimate of cost.

Available Model / Provider Selection

Depending on your plan you may be able to choose the model for a conversation, or the platform may pick one for you using routing rules set by administrators. Administrators may restrict models by plan, by department, by team, by individual and, for compliance, by provider region. A model that is not enabled for you cannot be used even if it exists in the platform. Some features, such as comparing two models, send your prompt to each selected model.

API-Based Processing

All AI processing happens through the providers’ APIs. The platform authenticates to each provider with credentials held by our administrators; those credentials are stored encrypted and are never shown to users, included in logs or exposed to your browser. Requests are made from our servers, not from your browser, so your browser does not talk to the providers directly.

User Prompts and Context

Everything relevant to your request can be sent to the provider: the prompt you type, the earlier messages in the same conversation that are needed for context, saved templates you insert, instructions set by your organisation and, when you use them, content from the files you attach. To keep requests within model limits and budgets the platform may shorten or summarise long conversation history before it is sent. Do not include information in a prompt that you are not permitted to share with a third-party AI provider.

Uploaded Documents / Files

Files you attach are checked for size, type and content safety, stored by the platform under your account, and, when you ask the AI to read or analyse them, converted to the text or image form the model needs and sent to the provider along with your prompt. Document reading, image reading, document generation, image or video generation and similar capabilities depend on your plan and on the provider and model used. Only upload files you have the right to use and that you accept being processed by the selected provider.

AI-Generated Responses

Responses are generated by the provider’s model. They are stored in your conversation, can be regenerated, rated, copied or exported (where your plan allows) and are treated like the rest of your conversation content. Responses are created automatically, are not reviewed by us before you see them, and may differ each time even for the same prompt.

Provider Limitations

Each provider and model has its own limits: maximum context length, supported languages and file types, knowledge cut-off, response speed, output length, and features that only some models support. A model may decline a request, may not know recent events and may behave differently after the provider updates it. The platform passes requests through and cannot remove these limitations.

Provider Availability

Because the providers run the models, our service depends on theirs. A provider can be slow, unavailable, change its pricing or policies, suspend or restrict our access, or retire a model. When a provider or model is unavailable, the platform shows an error or a temporary message and the affected features may not work until access returns. We do not guarantee that any given provider or model will be available at any time.

Provider-Specific Terms

Providers have their own terms of service, privacy policies, data-processing terms and usage policies, and those apply to how they handle the requests they receive from the platform. We do not reproduce or replace them. Please read them at the official sites linked above. If a provider’s terms change in a way that affects the service, we may change or remove the related features.

Provider Acceptable-Use Requirements

Providers prohibit certain uses of their models, typically including illegal activity, generating malware or deceptive content, harassment or hateful content, sexual content involving minors, weapons development, violating privacy or intellectual-property rights, and misleading people into thinking machine output is human-written where that matters. Your use of any model through the platform must comply with the acceptable-use policy of the provider that runs it as well as with our Terms. A provider may refuse or block a request, and we may suspend access if use breaches those rules.

Accuracy Limitations

AI models generate plausible text and can be wrong. They can state false information confidently, invent references, misread documents, make errors in calculations and code, reflect bias, and produce output that is incomplete or out of date. Output should be checked against reliable sources and, for important matters, by a qualified person. It is not legal, medical, financial, tax or other professional advice.

Confidentiality Considerations

Because content is sent to third-party providers, treat the service the way you would treat any external online tool. Avoid submitting secrets (passwords, keys, private credentials), highly sensitive personal data, information covered by a confidentiality duty you cannot delegate, or regulated data unless you have confirmed that doing so is permitted. Administrators can turn on scanning that warns about, redacts or blocks certain kinds of personal data (for example national ID numbers, payment card numbers and phone numbers) before a message is sent, and can restrict a department to providers in a chosen region; these are safeguards, not guarantees, and they do not detect every kind of sensitive information.

Model Changes

Models are updated, renamed and retired by their providers, and administrators can enable, disable or replace them. New models discovered from a provider are off until an administrator turns them on. A change can alter the style, accuracy, speed, cost or limits of the results you receive. We may change routing rules or default models without notice.

Provider Outages

If a provider has an outage, is rate-limited, or rejects requests, the affected requests fail or are delayed. The platform reports the problem to administrators through its status views but cannot repair a provider’s service. We do not automatically resend a request to a different provider unless an administrator has configured routing to do that, and doing so may send your content to another provider. Outages of a provider are not outages caused by us, and the Terms’ provisions on availability apply.

Rate Limits

To keep the service stable and fair, the platform limits how quickly requests can be made, how many new conversations can be started in a day and how many tokens and how much budget you can use in a day or month, according to your plan and any administrator overrides. Providers apply their own rate limits as well. When a limit is reached the request is refused with a message and can be retried later or after the allowance resets.

Safety Restrictions

Providers build safety controls into their models and may refuse, filter or alter responses. The platform adds its own controls, including plan-based feature access, file-type checks, optional personal-data scanning and administrator restrictions. Some requests may be refused even though they do not break our rules, because they conflict with a provider’s safety policy. Attempting to bypass safety restrictions is prohibited.
